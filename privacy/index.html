Privacy notice

How we look after your information

Last updated 24 November 2026.

This notice explains what personal information SimplicityIT (“we”, “us”) collects when you visit this website or get in touch, and what we do with it. We are the controller of this information. You can contact us at info@simplicitytech.co.uk.

This website

  • We do not use cookies, advertising trackers or analytics on this website.
  • There are no forms on this site. We do not collect anything from you just because you visit.
  • The fonts are served from this site, not from a third party.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. As the host, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests a page, under its own privacy statement. We do not receive that information.

When you email us

If you email us, we collect your name, your email address and anything else you choose to tell us. We use it to reply to you, to prepare a quote or proposal, and, if we work together, to provide and manage our services.

Our lawful basis is legitimate interests (answering the people who contact us) and, where you ask for a quote or become a customer, taking steps towards or performing a contract.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. Our email and other business systems are run by service providers who process information on our behalf and only as we instruct. We share information with others only where the law requires it or where you ask us to.

How long we keep it

We keep information for no longer than we need it. Enquiries that do not lead to work are deleted once we have dealt with them. If we work together, we keep records for the length of our relationship and for as long as the law requires afterwards.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to give you a copy of the information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, restrict how we use it, or stop using it for our legitimate interests. To do any of these, email info@simplicitytech.co.uk.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113. We would appreciate the chance to put things right first.

Changes to this notice

If we change how we use information, we will update this page and the date above.
